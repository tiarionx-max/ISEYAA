  <what-built>
    Complete Delivery Module — backend (DeliveryService with 12 methods + DeliveryGateway + DeliveryController) and mobile (Delivery tab 5 screens + Rider tab 5 screens).

    Key behaviors implemented:
    - Weight-based fee calculation (2 kg free allowance, per-kg surcharge above 2 kg)
    - 60-second rider matching via Redis GEOSEARCH on 'riders:online' geo-set
    - OTP sent via Termii to recipient's phone at order dispatch
    - Live GPS tracking via WebSocket ('delivery:' room prefix)
    - Dual-gate delivery completion: OTP verified by server + photo uploaded to S3/R2
    - 80% rider earnings / 20% platform split from 'delivery_platform_fee_pct' PlatformConfig row
    - ISY-RDR- reference prefix on wallet credit transactions
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Start backend and Expo Go with two devices (or two simulators). One device acts as Sender, one as Rider.

    **Step 1 — Confirm tabs visible:**
    Open app on both devices. Verify "Delivery" tab (Package icon) and "Rider" tab (Bike icon) appear in the tab bar.

    **Step 2 — Create a rider profile (Rider device):**
    Ensure the Rider device user has DRIVER role. Navigate to Rider tab → R-1 screen. Verify "Your KYC is under review" badge appears if not APPROVED.
    LGA Admin: call `PATCH /api/v1/delivery/riders/{id}/approve` with `{ "approved": true }` to approve the rider.

    **Step 3 — Rider goes online (Rider device):**
    Rider taps "GO ONLINE". Verify status dot turns green. Backend should receive `POST /delivery/go-online` and add rider to Redis 'riders:online' geo-set.

    **Step 4 — Sender requests delivery (Sender device):**
    Navigate to Delivery tab → D-1. Enter: pickup address, dropoff address, item description "Test parcel", weight "3" kg. Tap "Get Delivery Quote".
    Verify D-2 shows: base fee ₦300, weight surcharge > 0 (3 kg → 1 kg above free threshold → ₦50 surcharge), total = ₦350.
    Tap "Confirm Delivery". Verify D-3 matching screen appears with countdown from 60.

    **Step 5 — Rider receives request (Rider device):**
    Verify Rider tab shows incoming delivery card (R-2 screen) with: pickup address, item description, fee, 15-second timer bar.
    Tap "Accept". Verify card dismisses and Rider navigates to R-3 Pickup screen.

    **Step 6 — Live GPS tracking (both devices):**
    Verify Sender (D-4) shows rider marker on map. Move Rider device — verify marker updates within 3 seconds.
    Verify Delivery status shows "Picking Up" stage highlighted.

    **Step 7 — Parcel collected (Rider device):**
    Rider approaches pickup location within 200m. Verify "I've Collected" CTA activates (was previously disabled "Approach pickup point"). Tap "I've Collected". Verify Rider navigates to R-4 Active screen.

    **Step 8 — OTP entry (Rider device, R-4 Sub-state B):**
    Check SMS sent to recipient phone. If Termii not configured locally, check backend logs for "[TERMII STUB] Delivery OTP {OTP} for {phone}".
    Approach dropoff within 200m. Verify R-4 shows OTP input cells (sub-state B). Enter the 6-digit OTP from the logs. Verify cells turn green. Verify photo upload button activates.

    **Step 9 — Photo upload (Rider device):**
    Tap photo button. Select or take a test photo. Verify button shows thumbnail with green CheckCircle. Verify "Confirm Delivery" CTA activates (was previously disabled/grey).

    **Step 10 — Delivery confirmation (Rider device):**
    Tap "Confirm Delivery" → Alert shows "₦{amount} will be credited". Tap "Yes, Confirm". Verify credit banner appears with exact amount. Verify auto-navigate to R-5 Earnings screen after 2 seconds. Verify today's earnings show the credited amount.

    **Step 11 — Sender completion (Sender device):**
    Verify D-5 Complete screen shows: "Delivered!" heading, total fee, proof photo thumbnail. Rate the rider with 5 stars. Tap "Done".

    **Step 12 — Wallet verification:**
    Call `GET /api/v1/wallet/ledger` with rider JWT. Verify an entry with reference matching `ISY-RDR-*` and amount = 80% of delivery fee.
