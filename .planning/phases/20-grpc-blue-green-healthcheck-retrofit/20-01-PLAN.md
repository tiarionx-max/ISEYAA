<objective>
Retrofit `notifications-service` — the only live gRPC extraction (Phase 17) — with a real `grpc.health.v1.Health` endpoint on its gRPC port AND a plain HTTP `/healthz` sidecar Railway can actually poll (Railway's `healthcheckPath` is HTTP-only; it cannot probe gRPC/TCP directly — see 20-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 1). This turns Railway's rollout from a blunt "process didn't crash" check into a real health-gated promotion, satisfying GRPC-06a and giving GRPC-06c's blue-green cutover (20-05-PLAN.md) something concrete to gate on.

Purpose: Without this, a newly-promoted `notifications-service` container could be up-but-broken (e.g. failed to connect to Postgres) and Railway would still route traffic to it — defeating the entire point of a health-gated blue-green deploy.
Output: `notifications-service` bootstraps as a NestJS hybrid application (HTTP listener for `/healthz` + gRPC listener for business traffic, same process), `railway.toml` updated to poll it, and two new unit tests proving both health surfaces work.
</objective>
