<objective>
Extract News — the lowest-risk service in the D-05 risk-ascending rollout order (News -> Waitlist -> Reviews -> Delivery OTP) — into its own independently-deployable gRPC process, following the exact hybrid HTTP+gRPC pattern already live in `notifications-service`. `NewsService` itself is untouched; only the transport between `NewsController` and `NewsService` changes, from direct in-process injection to a `ClientGrpc` facade with a canary kill-switch.

Purpose: Prove the D-04/D-05 staggered rollout pattern on the simplest possible domain (pure read, no cross-domain writes, no shared mutable state) before Waitlist/Reviews/Delivery OTP follow the same shape with progressively more complexity.
Output: `backend/apps/news-service/` (new independently-buildable NestJS app) + `backend/src/modules/news-client/` (monolith-side facade) + updated `news.module.ts`/`news.controller.ts`/`app.module.ts` wiring.
</objective>
